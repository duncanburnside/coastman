### About

Dr Janine Hunter is a Researcher at the University of Dundee, UK, specialising in qualitative participatory methods, and primary and secondary qualitative analysis. Her works includes interdisciplinary projects around human interactions with the environment, refugees, youth transitions, environmental sustainability, knowledge exchange, participatory training and capacity strengthening. Chiefly working on projects in Africa, including Ghana, Uganda, Zimbabwe and DRC, Janine has contributed to numerous policy outputs, co-produced story maps, and academic articles on the lives of marginalised young people.
