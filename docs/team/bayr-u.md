### Position

- Researcher
- Norwegian Institute of Bioconomy Research (NIBIO), Norway

### About

Dr Bayr holds a PhD in Physical Geography from the University of Oslo (2021). Main fields of research are landscape ecology, land use change, vegetation science and biodiversity in cultural landscapes. Has experience in using GIS/RS for geospatial analysis and performing statistical analysis and programming in R. 

**Project management experience**: Dr Bayr has participated in different projects at NIBIO and is currently project leader of the project «light pollution and insects», financed by the Norwegian Road Authority. 

**Roles**: Bayr will actively participate in the activities of [WP1](/work-packages/wp1/) and [WP4](/work-packages/wp4/).
