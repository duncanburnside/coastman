### About

Dr. Alem has a Ph.D. and M.Sc. in Economics from the Norwegian University of Life Sciences (NMBU). Dr. Alem is a research scientist in the Department of Economics and Society at NIBIO since 2011. Previously worked as a researcher for the Ethiopian Institute of Agricultural Research. Dr. Alem has an impressive track record of over 20 years of valuable experience in applied research in Europe and Africa. 

**Project management experience**: Dr. Alem is the coordinator of the SYSTEMIC project (2019-2024), which has a primary focus on achieving food and nutrition security while considering the challenges posed by climate change in eight EU countries. Additionally, actively participates in other projects in Africa and EU. 

**Roles**: Play an active research role in [WP2](/work-packages/wp2/), will be involved in data analysis across the other work packages (WPs).
